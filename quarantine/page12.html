QUARANTINE is created by Alex Melvin. Each collage features assets from online games between 2007 & 2013 and interspersed with real life photography, including palettes from the Gameboy handheld system. The combination of the two creates a divide and then a collision between perceptions as the protagonist Jake struggles with his connection to Blue. In a world where more and more people are growing up online and finding their communities with countless others not bounded by location constraints, people are more lonely, more prone to isolation, and the nature of our connections ever more blurred. If you have ever had a friend you lost touch with online, and you can still see their old messages but are unable to contact them, this is for you.

assets from Minecraft, Roblox, Wizard101, Club Penguin, Poptropica, Runescape, Animal Jam, Flickr CC, and my own photography
